## Our Company

Ensemble is a San Francisco Bay Area software company founded in 2022 by three seasoned tech veterans on a mission to make enterprise-class application development dramatically easier, faster, and more resilient. Starting with a breakthrough low-code platform, the team pivoted in 2024 to address an even bigger enterprise challenge — delivering personalized, AI-powered experiences at scale.

Since launch, Ensemble has signed multiple enterprise customers — several in active pilots and others already in production — with early deployments delivering massive ROI.

AI Concierge Platform · Launched August 2025

The Ensemble AI Concierge Platform lets enterprises build, deploy, and continuously improve AI agents that serve stakeholders across any channel — connecting to internal knowledge bases, SOPs, and enterprise systems through a no-code interface.

- Enterprise integrations — Deep enterprise system connections, secure credential management, and audit logging
- Knowledge & RAG — Retrieval over internal docs, SOPs, and knowledge bases
- Hybrid reasoning — Deterministic + LLM reasoning for reliable, production-grade workflows
- Continuous improvement — Built-in feedback loop for ongoing agent refinement over time
