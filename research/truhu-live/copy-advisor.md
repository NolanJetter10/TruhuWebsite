## 8. FAQ — "Frequently Asked Questions" (5 category cards; click to expand Q&A)
Sub: "Everything brokers need to know about TruHu, from platform capabilities to compliance, results, and more."

### PLATFORM — What TruHu Does
- **What is TruHu Advisor?** — "TruHu Advisor is a benefits communication and engagement platform that helps brokers communicate clearly and consistently with their clients' employees. We build branded benefit websites for your clients, run multichannel enrollment campaigns via text and email, create AI-powered benefit guides, and provide compliance tracking — all managed by our team, not yours. Think of it as your communications department for every group in your book."
- **What's included — is this just a benefits website?** — "No. TruHu is a hub of resources that includes the full communication stack: text + email messaging, benefit guide creation, employee surveys, acknowledgment tracking for compliance, translation to 100+ languages, and engagement analytics showing exactly who opened and when."
- **Does TruHu create AI-powered benefit guides?** — "Yes. AI is used to generate personalized, easy-to-understand benefit guides. These guides are branded for each client and designed to help employees make better benefit decisions."
- **Who builds everything, my team or yours?** — "Our team handles everything. You complete a short intake form; we build the site and campaigns for you. Projects are completed within 5 days, often within 24 hours of receiving the intake form."
- **How do you track employee engagement?** — "Each employee who received, opened, clicked, and completed each message or task, is shown in the platform, giving brokers and employers clear visibility into what messages are working."
- **What does TruHu Advisor cost?** — "Under $300 per employer, per year for platform access— site, campaigns, surveys, compliance tracking, benefit guides. Volume discounts apply."
- **Who pays — the broker or the employer?** — "For TruHu Advisor, the broker covers the cost. Most partners position it as a free value-add to their clients — when the employer hears \"this is included through your broker,\" adoption happens naturally. For TruHu Workforce (our employer-operated platform for year-round communication), employers can be referred to TruHu for a discounted rate through their broker partnership. Many brokers choose to cover some or all of the Workforce cost as well, depending on their partnership level with TruHu."

### BROKER VALUE — Why Brokers Use It
- **How does TruHu help me differentiate my agency?** — "TruHu lets you deliver a consistent, branded communication experience to every client, not just your largest accounts. That consistency across your book is a clear service differentiator and raises the standard for every client relationship."
- **How does TruHu help with client retention?** — "TruHu becomes part of how your clients communicate year-round, from benefits to safety, onboarding and policy updates. That makes it embedded in their day-to-day operations and creates meaningful switching cost at renewal."
- **How do my account managers benefit?** — "The platform reduces the volume of routine questions by giving employees easy access to their information. This frees up your team during busy seasons and allows them to focus more on strategy and client support."
- **Can I deploy this across my entire book of business?** — "Yes. TruHu is designed to scale across your entire book, with a consistent process and experience for every client, regardless of size."
- **How does TruHu show measurable value to my clients?** — "TruHu provides clear engagement data, showing who received, opened, and interacted with communications. This gives you a simple way to demonstrate value and impact to your clients."

### EMPLOYEE EXPERIENCE — How It Works for Employees
- **Will employees engage? A lot of our clients' workforce doesn't check email.** — "Yes. Results vary based on four factors: industry, company culture, workforce composition, and existing systems. We offer a fit assessment to help you understand what to expect for specific clients. In industries like transportation and manufacturing, we consistently see 50%+ increase in engagement because the delivery method matches how the workforce truly communicates."
- **Do employees need to download an app or log in?** — "No. Employees receive messages directly by text or email, with no app to download and no login required."
- **How does TruHu support multilingual workforces?** — "Messages are automatically translated into 100+ languages using content-aware AI designed to handle benefits and insurance terminology. Each employee receives communication in their preferred language, helping ensure clarity across diverse workforces."
- **Does TruHu work alongside our clients' enrollment system?** — "Yes. TruHu makes enrollment easier — not by replacing the enrollment platform, but by driving employees to it. Employees receive mobile-friendly benefit summaries they can share with their spouse or family, then navigate directly through to the enrollment system. Brokers tell us participation improves and employees complete enrollment faster because they understand their options before they log in."

### SECURITY — Compliance & Security
- **Is TruHu SOC 2 compliant?** — "Yes. TruHu operates under SOC 2 protocols, which means your data — and your employees' data — is protected by rigorous, independently validated security controls. This isn't just a checkbox for us; it's the foundation of how we build and operate the platform. If you'd like details on what our compliance covers, we're happy to walk you through it."
- **How does TruHu handle employee consent for text messaging?** — "TruHu makes it easy to handle consent the right way for your workforce. We support both explicit opt-in and compliant implied consent approaches. Employers can choose the method that fits their policies, with templates and guidance provided. Messages are sent by the employer, not TruHu, which is an important distinction for compliance."
- **How is employee data protected?** — "Sites use unique, non-indexed URLs that don't appear in search engines. Employee data is handled under SOC 2 protocols. For confidential documents, TruHu's acknowledgment tracking creates a compliance-grade audit trail that records exactly who accessed what, and when — giving HR and brokers the documentation they need without any manual tracking. For organizations handling proprietary or restricted content, TruHu also supports password-gated access — an additional authentication layer that controls who can view a document or page, independent of how the link was shared."

### RESULTS — Results
- **What results should I expect?** — "Every workforce is different, and results reflect that — industry, company culture, workforce composition, and existing communication habits all play a role. That's why we offer a fit assessment that helps predict what engagement will look like for your specific client base before you commit. Across the platform, we consistently see text message click rates that significantly outperform industry averages, companies completing open enrollment ahead of deadline, and compliance-grade records for every communication sent. One pattern stands out — accounts where messaging comes from multiple people (not just HR, but supervisors and team leads too) see meaningfully higher employee engagement. Employees respond when the communication feels close to home. The result brokers notice most? Client retention is enhanced. When employees rely on TruHu as their go-to source for benefits and workplace communications, switching brokers means leaving behind the system their people already use and trust."
