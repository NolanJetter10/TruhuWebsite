# Team feedback on the new homepage (Oct 6, 2026)

**Collected:** October 6, 2026, from Khurram, Xander, Satchel and Carolyn, on the new homepage draft.
**Attribution:** each item names who raised it. When two people made the same point, both are named. The original notes are at the bottom.

## What's working
- The new main page is better than the current site (Khurram)
- The overall direction is right (Satchel)
- The copy looks and sounds good overall and needs only minor wording fixes (Xander)
- The animations and small touches, especially the pixel trail that follows the cursor (Xander, Carolyn)
- The product demonstrations (Carolyn)

## Positioning and story
- The page sells *less work* (faster, cheaper) but never says the *result* is far better than what a broker could produce by hand. Present TruHu as a competitive advantage that helps brokers grow their top line, not only as a cost saving. Companies that compete only on cost play a brutal game and most die. Strategic partners that help a customer's top and bottom lines thrive. His restaurant analogy: the pitch shouldn't stop at "fast without compromising quality." It should also say "the best food, everything on the menu, quality you can't imagine," all fast and cheap (Khurram)
- "AI turns documents into guides" is already an old story that many startups in the space tell. The site has to lead with what's different (Khurram)
- Don't over-dramatize the problems, because real problems speak for themselves (Carolyn)
- Don't lose the (truly) human aspect. TruHu is a tech company, but the industry runs on personal relationships, so show real people (Carolyn, Satchel)

## Capabilities to feature
- Show off the finished guides and benefits sites, which are beautiful (Khurram)
- Translations into many languages in seconds (Khurram)
- Multiple ways to present: "fills" (word unclear, possibly flipbooks), videos and presentations (Khurram)
- The AI design assistant, which is unique to TruHu and heavily used (Khurram)
- Accuracy (Khurram)
- The benefits assistant. He expects it to become a key highlight, and it supports the case that the outcome is much better, not just faster and cheaper to build (Khurram)
- Narration, plus the ability to ask questions partway through it. This is something TruHu can claim to have pioneered (Khurram)

## Specific fixes on the current page
- Line up the problem boxes and remove the wobble/skew on the tags in those blocks, because it clashes with the clean, modern look (Xander, Carolyn). *Context: the misalignment was meant to make the problems feel chaotic. Since several people dislike it, the plan is to remove it.*
- Remove "sample data" from the deliverables tag in the step-by-step section (Xander)
- Replace "Q&A" with explicit chatbot and video wording, since "Q&A" could be misread (Xander)
- Add "flipbook" to the deliverables block so the set stays symmetrical (Xander)
- Don't use real URLs or real companies as examples (e.g. ridgeline.com). Use generic or made-up companies unless there's signed authorization, especially when the link leads to a VC (Carolyn)

## Reference sites to study (Satchel)
- **Outmarket's benefits page** (outmarket.ai/platform/benefits): it reads like a product demo and isn't afraid to show what's under the hood
- **FurtherAI** (furtherai.com): it looks professional, and its imagery signals that the company works in the biggest markets with real people
